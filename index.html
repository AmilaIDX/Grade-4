ABHIPPRA SPEAKER 01

Kindness Makes a Difference.

“

A little kindness can make a big difference.

What Happened in the Hallway:

When books tumbled across the floor, running past would have been easy. But taking ten seconds to bend down, pick them up, and offer a smile turned an embarrassing moment into an inspiring memory.

✓ Kindness costs nothing, but it is worth everything.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
PESHALA SPEAKER 02

Fairness for Everyone.

“

Everyone deserves a fair chance.

On the Sussex Playground:

True sportsmanship isn’t just about who scores the winning goal. It's making sure no friend sits on the bench wishing they were picked. When everyone plays, everyone wins!

✓ Pass the ball, share the space, welcome everyone.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
THISENI SPEAKER 03

Honesty Is the Best Policy.

“

Always choose the truth.

Finding the Lost Pencil Case:

A beautiful sparkling pencil case was left behind on a desk. It was tempting to keep, but honesty reminded Thiseni that someone was searching for it with tears. Returning it brought pure joy to both.

✓ Trust is built in seconds and treasured for life.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
BHANUJA SPEAKER 04

Respect Makes Us Better.

“

Listen, care and respect everyone.

In Our Grade 4 Classroom:

Respect is quiet patience when someone else is talking. It is listening not just with our ears, but with an open heart. When we show respect to our teachers and peers, knowledge flourishes.

✓ Treat others the way you want to be treated.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
YENUTH SPEAKER 05

Be Kind to Animals.

“

Every living creature deserves kindness.

In Our Sussex Gardens:

Animals cannot ask for a drink of water or tell us when they are scared. When we put out water for birds, gently pet a puppy, or protect a sleeping kitten, we protect the beautiful nature of our planet.

✓ Gentle hands and caring hearts heal the earth.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
DEWLI SPEAKER 06

Stand Up for What Is Right.

“

Be brave. Be kind. Make things right.

Being a Real Upstander:

It only takes one courageous voice to break the silence. When someone is teased or left out, stepping forward and saying “Come sit with me” is the most powerful superpower any student possesses.

✓ Justice starts when ordinary kids choose to be brave.
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
