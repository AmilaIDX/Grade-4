ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 01 ABHIPPRA

Kindness Makes a Difference

“

Kindness is contagious! When we are kind to others, they may be kind to others too.

✨A simple smile or helping hand can make someone happy.
🤝Never laugh at others or hurt someone's feelings.
🌱Even a small act of kindness makes a big difference.
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 02 PESHALA

Fairness for Everyone

“

Justice begins when we treat others fairly. Every child deserves respect, friendship, and love.

⚖️Treat everyone equally and give everyone a fair chance.
🏀Never leave anyone out of a game because they are different.
🌟Share, take turns, and welcome all classmates into the circle.
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 03 THISENI

Honesty Is the Best Policy

“

Honesty means telling the truth and doing the right thing, even when nobody is watching.

💎Telling the truth builds lifelong trust with friends and teachers.
🛡️When we make a mistake, admit it and try to correct it.
✨Be truthful in our words and actions every single day.
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 04 BHANUJA

Respect Makes Us Better

“

When we show respect, we help make our school and our world a happier place.

👂Listen attentively when others speak and use polite words.
🤝Accept that people may have different, beautiful ideas.
🏫Respect parents, teachers, friends, and living creatures.
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 05 YENUTH

Be Kind to Animals

“

Animals are living beings, and they need our love and care. Animals deserve kindness too!

🐾Animals cannot tell us when they are hungry or frightened.
💧Offer food and clean water, and never tease or hurt them.
🕊️Always ask an adult to help an animal in danger.
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 06 DEWLI

Stand Up for What Is Right

“

Being brave does not mean fighting. It means choosing to do the right thing, even when it is difficult.

🛡️When we see someone left out or bullied, never laugh or walk away.
🤝Support them with kind words and ask a trusted adult for help.
🌟Let us be brave, be fair, and stand up for justice!
