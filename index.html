Introduction & Opening Address

Good morning, respected Principal Sir, Vice Principal Madam, teachers, and my dear friends.

Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 01 ABHIPPRA
Kindness Makes a Difference
“

Let me begin with my speech on “Kindness Makes a Difference.”

Kindness means being helpful, caring, and friendly to others. A simple smile, a kind word, or a helping hand can make someone happy. We should never laugh at others or hurt their feelings. Even a small act of kindness can make a big difference.

Remember, kindness is contagious! When we are kind to others, they may be kind to others too.

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 02 PESHALA
Fairness for Everyone
“

Fairness means treating everyone equally and giving everyone a fair chance. We should not leave anyone out of a game or treat someone badly because they are different from us. Every child deserves respect, friendship, and love.

Let us share, take turns, and include everyone. Remember, justice begins when we treat others fairly.

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 03 THISENI
Honesty Is the Best Policy
“

Honesty means telling the truth and doing the right thing, even when nobody is watching. Sometimes, telling the truth may be difficult, but it helps others trust us. If we make a mistake, we should admit it and try to correct it.

Let us always be truthful in our words and actions because honesty makes us better people.

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 04 BHANUJA
Respect Makes Us Better
“

Respect means treating others with kindness and care. We should respect our parents, teachers, friends, and everyone around us. We must listen when others speak, use polite words, and accept that people may have different ideas.

We should also respect animals and never hurt them. When we show respect, we help make our school and our world a happier place.

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 05 YENUTH
Be Kind to Animals
“

Animals are living beings, and they need our love and care. They cannot always tell us when they are hungry, hurt, or frightened. So, we must be gentle with them and never tease, hit, or hurt them.

We can help animals by giving them food and water when appropriate and by asking an adult to help an animal in danger. Remember, animals deserve kindness too!

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • SPEAKER 06 DEWLI
Stand Up for What Is Right
“

Sometimes, we may see someone being bullied, left out, or treated unfairly. We should not laugh or simply walk away. We can support that person, speak kindly, and ask a trusted adult for help.

Being brave does not mean fighting. It means choosing to do the right thing, even when it is difficult. Let us be brave, be fair, and stand up for justice!

Thank you!
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
ITEM 01 • FINAL MESSAGE All six children together, loudly and confidently:

“Kindness is contagious, and justice starts with us!”

Thank you!
Abhippra Peshala Thiseni Bhanuja Yenuth Dewli
Class Teacher: Darshani Gunathilaka
Sussex College Grade 4 Assembly
