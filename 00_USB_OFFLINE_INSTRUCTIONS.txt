HOW TO COPY AND USE THIS FOLDER ON A USB DRIVE (OFFLINE BACKUP GUIDE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. HOW TO COPY TO YOUR USB DRIVE:
   - Insert your USB Flash Drive into your computer.
   - Right-click this entire folder ("Grade 4 Assembly") and select "Copy".
   - Open your USB Flash Drive and select "Paste".
   - Wait for the copy to finish. That's it!

2. HOW TO LAUNCH AT SCHOOL (NO INTERNET NEEDED):
   - Insert your USB Flash Drive into the school laptop or projector PC.
   - Open the "Grade 4 Assembly" folder on the USB drive.
   - DOUBLE-CLICK:
       ▶ "start_presentation.bat"  (One-click launcher for Windows)
       OR
       ▶ "index.html" (or "START_HERE.html")
   - The presentation will open immediately in your web browser (Chrome, Edge,
     Firefox, Brave, etc.).
   - NO WI-FI OR INTERNET CONNECTION IS REQUIRED.
     All 37 slides, artwork, photos, animations, and sound effects are stored
     locally inside this folder.

3. KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS DURING THE ASSEMBLY:
   - [Spacebar] or [Right Arrow ->] : Next Slide
   - [Left Arrow <-] or [Backspace] : Previous Slide
   - [1] to [6]                     : Jump directly to Assembly Items 1 - 6
   - [F]                            : Toggle Fullscreen Mode (1920x1080)
   - [N]                            : Open Teacher Rehearsal Notes & Verbatim Scripts
   - [O]                            : Open 37-Slide Overview Grid
   - [C]                            : Trigger Confetti Celebration (Final Slide)
   - [Esc]                          : Close Drawer / Modal / Fullscreen

4. AUDIO / SOUND SETTINGS:
   - Sound is set to OFF by default so it will not interrupt speech unexpectedly.
   - To turn sound ON, click the Speaker icon in the top navigation bar.

5. ITEM BREAKDOWN (ALL 37 SLIDES):
   - Slide 01       : Assembly Welcome & Title
   - Slides 02 - 08 : Item 01: Six Voices, One Message (Speeches & Grand Chorus)
   - Slides 09 - 13 : Item 02: A Little Smile & Care (Group Poem & Recitations)
   - Slides 14 - 21 : Item 03: If Only Someone Had Spoken (Playground Role-Play)
   - Slides 22 - 32 : Item 04: Justice Detectives (10 Everyday Case Exhibits)
   - Slide 33       : Item 05: Video Presentation (Kindness & Justice in Action)
   - Slides 34 - 37 : Item 06: Our Kindness & Justice Pledge & Grand Celebration Finale

6. VIDEO PRESENTATION (SLIDE 33) OFFLINE TIP:
   - If connected to the internet, the embedded YouTube player works automatically.
   - If completely offline without internet, you can optionally place an MP4 file
     named "assembly_video.mp4" into this folder, and it will play locally!

7. POWERPOINT (.PPTX) BACKUP:
   - Click the "PowerPoint" button in the bottom HUD bar at any time to export
     the entire presentation as an editable 37-slide .pptx presentation with
     all verbatim student scripts embedded in the Speaker Notes!
